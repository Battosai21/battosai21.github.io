Welcome, dear stranger (or not) to my personal website! This site is intended to share my journey through life and experiences, so that when future generations of my family look back on my life, they will have a clear picture of who I was and what I stood for. As the famous saying goes: 'We all die twice: the first time is when we simply cease to be; the second time is when we are forgotten'. Since I find too difficult to remember every detail of my story in a normal conversation, I thought I might put everything in one place, so that people can easily find and read about me. I hope it's not too boring. There is a conveniently placed timeline above this text so you can jump between years if you don't want to read the whole thing. I would like to thank you from the bottom of my heart for visiting my site. I hope you find something interesting! If you would like to see a more professional version of my story, please check out my resume (CV) instead.

2014

Joining the Argentine Navy Reserve

Started as a reservist in the Argentine Navy, training in hazmat/dangerous goods handling, firefighting, and combat medic skills — a foundation of procedure and discipline that still shows up in how I approach ramp safety today.

2017

Writing software for the first time professionally

Joined Brain Knowledge Systems in Argentina as a developer, working in Python, Django, and C. This is where the engineering side of things started — a thread that later became automation scripts, side projects, and eventually freelance work.

2019

HR reporting at Accenture

Moved into HR report writing at Accenture Argentina, working with Workday. Less code, more data and process — but it sharpened how I think about structuring information clearly for other people to use.

2021

Moving to Japan

Relocated to Sapporo, Hokkaido. New language environment, new career direction — the start of the aviation ground operations chapter.

2022

Ramp agent at Skymark Airlines

Began working ramp and ground operations at New Chitose Airport — baggage handling, aircraft turnaround, and the procedural rhythm of airside work.

2024

Ramp agent at FRS

Continued in ground operations at FRS, taking on weight and balance responsibilities alongside ramp safety work.

2026

Building things on the side

Shipped a METAR/TAF weather app for pilots, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, and started taking on freelance Python and automation work — bringing the software side back into the picture alongside aviation.
